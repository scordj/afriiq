Rules

  • 10 questions, four options each, 10 seconds per question.
  • Correct answer: 100 points. Quick correct answers earn up to 50 bonus points. The most you can score is 1,500.
  • Wrong or unanswered: 0 points. Your first tap is final.
  • The Daily Challenge uses the same questions for everyone on a given date.
  • No entry fee. No cash prizes in this prototype.

Privacy

This prototype keeps your nickname, best score and badges in your own browser only. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The leaderboard players are sample data, not real users.
